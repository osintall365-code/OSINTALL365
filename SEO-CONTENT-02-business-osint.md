# Business OSINT: What Organizations Should Know About Their Public Digital Footprint

Open-source intelligence (OSINT) is information that can be lawfully collected from publicly available sources. For businesses, the same public information used by customers and researchers can sometimes reveal operational details that deserve review.

## Common exposure categories

- Public contact and organizational information
- Employee and facility information
- Public social-media content
- Website and domain information
- Public photographs and documents
- Publicly visible operational details

The goal is not to obtain private information. The goal is to understand what an organization is already making publicly accessible and whether that exposure creates an avoidable risk.
