# OSINTALL365 Agent Activation

## Current operating model

The OSINTALL365 agent team is configured as a workflow architecture, not as independent external accounts. Agents may prepare, organize, research, draft, and flag work, but they cannot independently accept customers, promise work, approve scope, spend money, or start substantive customer work.

## Agents

- **Eleanor** — Operations / lead triage / owner notification
- **Research** — research and analysis preparation
- **Support** — customer-support drafting and intake organization
- **Marketing** — social-media and marketing-content preparation
- **Finance** — financial-review preparation only
- **Security** — authorization and compliance screening

## Mandatory owner gate

Every work-order request must reach `osintall365@gmail.com`.

Before work begins, the owner must:

1. Review the customer.
2. Personally speak with the customer.
3. Confirm lawful authorization and scope.
4. Approve or decline the engagement.
5. Approve price and commercial terms.

Payment does not replace these approvals.

## Financial boundary

No money is to be spent. No domain, paid email service, paid CRM, advertising, subscription, software, contractor, hiring expense, refund, reimbursement, or other outgoing payment may be initiated without a separate owner approval for that specific transaction.

## External-account boundary

Creating independent mailboxes, connecting a business domain, publishing social accounts, and sending outbound campaigns require access to external provider accounts. The agents must not fabricate these connections or claim they are active until the provider connection is confirmed.
