Business OSINT: What Organizations Should Know About Their Public Digital Footprint

Open-source intelligence, or OSINT, is the structured collection and analysis of information that is lawfully available to the public. For organizations, that information can reveal a digital footprint worth understanding before it becomes a security problem.

What does business OSINT examine?

A business-focused review may examine public websites, domain information, public-facing contact details, social-media exposure, employee or facility information, published documents, and other openly accessible organizational information.

Why does a public footprint matter?

Individual pieces of information may appear routine. When combined, however, they can reveal operational patterns, relationships, facilities, technology choices, or other details an organization may prefer to control. A structured review helps separate ordinary public information from avoidable exposure.
