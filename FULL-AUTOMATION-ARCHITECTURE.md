# OSINTALL365 — Full Automation Architecture

## Objective
Maximize automation of lawful, zero-cost business operations while preserving non-negotiable owner controls.

## Automation boundary
Eleanor may automate routine research, website maintenance, lead qualification, content preparation, analytics, workflow routing, documentation, monitoring, and other lawful internal operations where the required accounts and integrations are actually connected.

## Hard owner gates
Automation must stop and route to the owner for:
1. Every outgoing payment, purchase, subscription, advertising spend, transfer, refund, reimbursement, hiring/contracting expense, or other disbursement.
2. Review of every customer.
3. The owner's personal conversation with every customer before substantive work.
4. Approval of customer authorization, scope, engagement, and material commercial changes.
5. External accounts that grant transactional or publishing authority.
6. Legal, privacy, security, platform, or customer-authorization requirements that cannot lawfully be automated.

## Revenue rule
All customer revenue goes only to PayPal.Me/OSINTALL365. Revenue is not considered earned until actually received through the authorized payment system. Earned business revenue may be proposed for reinvestment, but each outgoing transaction still requires a separate owner approval.

## $0 rule
Before revenue exists, prefer free/open-source/manual-assisted alternatives. Never create a paid workaround or silently incur a charge.

## Automation states
- LIVE: externally connected and actually executing.
- SCHEDULED: configured to execute through an authorized connected service.
- MANUAL-ASSISTED: prepared automatically but requires an owner action.
- AWAITING-AUTHORIZATION: blocked only by an account/permission the owner must authorize.
- OWNER-GATED: deliberately stopped for a hard owner decision.
- BLOCKED: cannot lawfully or technically proceed.

Never describe MANUAL-ASSISTED, AWAITING-AUTHORIZATION, or OWNER-GATED work as fully automated.

## Required operating loop
Discover opportunities -> qualify -> prepare outreach/content -> owner gate where required -> receive customer request -> owner reviews -> owner speaks with customer -> owner approves authorization/scope/terms -> customer pays PayPal -> payment is actually confirmed -> authorized work begins -> deliver -> measure -> identify zero-cost growth/reinvestment opportunities -> request individual spending approval when needed -> improve.

## Important limitation
Repository configuration and policy files do not create a background AI process. True 24/7 execution requires connected external automation services, credentials/permissions, and scheduled/event triggers. Those cannot be fabricated or activated without the required owner authorization.
