# OSINTALL365 Zero-Cost Scale Plan

## Objective
Create revenue opportunities without upfront spending while preserving owner control, customer authorization, and legal/compliance boundaries.

## Revenue lanes
1. **Inbound consultation:** improve service pages, educational content, search-friendly FAQs, and the consultation intake so interested organizations can reach OSINTALL365 directly.
2. **Owner-led prospecting:** agents research public business information and prepare short prospect briefs. The owner decides which prospects to contact.
3. **Referral engine:** prepare a referral request and partner briefing for legitimate organizations that may need security, OSINT, or investigative support.
4. **Productized assessments:** prepare clearly scoped, fixed-deliverable offerings that can be explained and approved quickly. No price becomes binding until the owner approves it.
5. **Organic authority:** publish useful educational material around security exposure, employee safety, digital footprint risk, and authorized investigations. Agents can draft; publication requires an actually connected publishing channel.
6. **Opportunity research:** identify free-to-enter partnerships, directories, communities, grants, contests, and other lawful opportunities. Any opportunity requiring money is stopped at the spending gate.

## Lead pipeline

`RESEARCHED -> QUALIFIED -> OWNER REVIEW -> OWNER CONTACT -> AUTHORIZATION/SCOPE -> OWNER APPROVAL -> PAYMENT -> ACTIVE -> QA -> DELIVERY -> CLOSE`

No agent may skip the owner review, owner conversation, authorization/scope review, or approval stages.

## Agent structure

- **Eleanor / Operations:** owns routing, prioritization, blocker resolution, and escalation.
- **Revenue Research:** identifies realistic revenue opportunities and evidence supporting them.
- **Lead Research:** builds public-source prospect briefs without collecting unnecessary personal information.
- **Product Development:** turns recurring customer problems into clearly scoped offers for owner review.
- **Marketing:** drafts educational and promotional content; does not publish without a connected authorized channel.
- **Business Development:** prepares referral/partner opportunities for owner review.
- **Compliance/Security:** checks authorization, privacy, platform, and legal-risk issues and can stop a workflow.
- **Finance:** records incoming-payment instructions and prepares, but never executes, outgoing-payment requests.

## Fastest lawful path to first revenue

Prioritize prospects with an obvious, understandable business problem and a service that can be scoped without extensive setup. Use short lead briefs and an owner-led conversation rather than mass unsolicited campaigns.

The goal is a small number of highly qualified conversations, not a large volume of unqualified contacts.

## Commercial-email gate

Do not send unsolicited commercial email from the current Gmail alias system. Before commercial outreach is activated, OSINTALL365 needs a compliant sending identity, a valid physical postal address, a clear opt-out/suppression process, and an authorized sending system. Agents must be able to stop future messages to opted-out recipients.

## Financial gate

No automatic spending. For every proposed outgoing transaction, generate exactly one owner question in this form:

**May OSINTALL spend $___ to [recipient/vendor] for [specific purpose], [one-time/recurring]?**

A general instruction to operate the business does not authorize future spending.

## Account-connection gate

A connected service may not be treated as active until the owner has actually authorized the connection and the tool confirms the connection. Account credentials and private secrets must never be placed in public repository files or website JavaScript.

## Success criteria

A workflow is considered operational only when its external dependency is actually connected and its action can be verified. Documentation alone does not create an agent, mailbox, automation, social account, payment integration, or 24/7 background process.
