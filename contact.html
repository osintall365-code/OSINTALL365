PAYMENT & SPENDING CONTROL

Incoming funds may be received; outgoing funds require owner approval.

OSINTALL365 may receive customer payments through its designated PayPal payment link. No agent, workflow, or automated process is authorized to spend, transfer, refund, reimburse, subscribe, purchase, hire, or otherwise disburse business funds without the owner's separate approval for that specific outgoing transaction.

Before any outgoing transaction is made, the owner must receive one clear question stating the exact amount, recipient/vendor, purpose, and whether the transaction is one-time or recurring. No approval is implied by silence, prior approval of another expense, or general authorization.

Do not send passwords, payment-card numbers, Social Security numbers, confidential credentials, classified information, restricted law-enforcement information, or unnecessary personal information. Digital testing and diagnostics are performed only within an authorized scope.

If a request involves unauthorized access, credential theft, hacking without permission, stalking, harassment, doxxing, restricted databases, or unauthorized surveillance, OSINTALL365 will not perform the activity.
