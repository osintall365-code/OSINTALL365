# What Is a Digital Exposure Review?

Businesses can unintentionally publish information that increases operational and security risk. A digital exposure review examines lawful, publicly available information connected to an organization and identifies practical issues that deserve attention.

## What can be reviewed?

A focused review can examine public-facing contact information, employee or facility exposure, social-media exposure, website and domain information, and other publicly visible organizational details.

## Why does it matter?

Information that appears harmless by itself can become more useful when combined with other public information. Organizations can reduce avoidable exposure by identifying what is visible, deciding what should remain public, and prioritizing reasonable corrective actions.
