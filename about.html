ABOUT OSINTALL

Investigative discipline applied to public information.

OSINTALL is a public-source intelligence research company focused on turning open information into organized, decision-ready findings.

The company is led by Derek Paul McNamara, a law-enforcement professional with experience as a police officer, criminal investigator, and Major Crimes Task Force investigator. His professional background includes investigative research, digital evidence, electronic communications, homicide investigations, narcotics investigations, and other complex criminal investigations.

OSINTALL keeps that investigative mindset while maintaining a clear boundary: customer work is based on lawful, publicly available information and does not provide unauthorized access to private or restricted systems.

PROFESSIONAL BACKGROUND

Relevant experience

Professional training and experience include APOST certification, NCIC certification, advanced narcotics investigations, open-source intelligence, digital forensics, violent-crime investigations, threat assessment, digital evidence preservation, and investigations involving electronic communications and cellular devices.

Recognition includes 2025 Officer of the Year and a 2025 commendation from the Citronelle Police Department.

Professional credentials describe the owner's background and do not mean OSINTALL customers receive access to restricted government databases or law-enforcement systems.
