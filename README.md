# OSINTALL365

Public-source intelligence, security analysis, and investigative support business system.

## Zero-budget operating plan

- No purchases, subscriptions, paid advertising, domains, hosting upgrades, or other outgoing spending without a separate owner approval.
- Existing free infrastructure is used only as an interim/staging environment.
- Future business domain is reserved conceptually for the OSINTALL365 brand; no domain purchase is authorized under the $0 budget.
- Future agent mailboxes will use the approved business domain and forward required owner notifications to `osintall365@gmail.com`.

## Owner-first customer control

Every work-order request must reach the owner. Agents may triage, organize, summarize, flag compliance issues, and prepare a customer briefing, but they may not accept a customer or begin substantive work.

Before any engagement begins, the owner must:

1. Review the customer.
2. Personally speak with the customer.
3. Review the requested scope and authorization.
4. Explicitly approve or decline the engagement.
5. Approve the commercial terms.

Payment does not replace owner approval.

## Financial control

Incoming customer payments may be received through the designated OSINTALL365 PayPal destination. Every outgoing payment, purchase, subscription, refund, reimbursement, transfer, advertising expense, hiring expense, or other disbursement requires a separate owner approval stating the exact amount, recipient/vendor, purpose, and whether it is one-time or recurring.

## Security and authorization

Services are limited to lawful, authorized activity. No unauthorized access to private or restricted systems, credential theft, stalking, harassment, doxxing, illegal surveillance, or access to restricted law-enforcement systems is offered.

## Hosting note

GitHub Pages is currently being used as the available free staging environment. GitHub's current terms state that Pages is not intended to operate a free-hosted online business primarily facilitating commercial transactions or SaaS. Before OSINTALL365 relies on the site for production business operations, it should be migrated to a suitable free commercial-capable host such as Cloudflare Pages, subject to account setup and confirmation that the required free tier remains available. Cloudflare currently documents a Free Pages plan with free static-asset requests. No paid Cloudflare plan is authorized.
