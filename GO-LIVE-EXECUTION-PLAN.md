# OSINTALL365 — Eleanor Go-Live Execution Plan

## Objective
Move OSINTALL365 from documented operating design toward a real, lawful, zero-cost production operation without falsely claiming external services are connected.

## Eleanor's execution rule
Eleanor should independently complete every step that can be completed with existing authorized tools and $0 resources. When a step requires an external account authorization, credential, deployment approval, legal/business address, or transactional/publishing authority, Eleanor must stop at that boundary and give the owner the exact action required.

## Go-live gates
### Gate 1 — Public website
- Maintain the existing website as the staging/public review environment.
- Do not represent GitHub Pages as the permanent commercial production host.
- Production migration target: a free static host suitable for the business, subject to owner account authorization.
- Verify index, navigation, mobile layout, contact workflow, privacy, terms, and payment destination before production launch.

### Gate 2 — Customer intake
- Every work-order request must reach the owner.
- Intake must not itself accept an engagement or authorize substantive work.
- Current fallback must remain available until a server-side intake service is actually deployed and tested.
- If Google Apps Script is deployed, test successful delivery, failure handling, required fields, and owner routing before treating it as live.

### Gate 3 — Revenue
- All customer/business revenue routes only to PayPal.Me/OSINTALL365.
- No alternate processor or payment destination.
- No revenue is counted until actually received and verified through an authorized payment system.
- Payment never authorizes work.

### Gate 4 — Earned-revenue reinvestment
- No spending before revenue is actually generated and received.
- Once earned business funds exist, Eleanor may identify revenue-producing reinvestment opportunities.
- Every actual outgoing transaction still requires the owner's separate explicit approval with amount, recipient/vendor, purpose, and one-time/recurring status.

### Gate 5 — Customer authorization
Before substantive work:
1. Owner reviews the request.
2. Owner personally speaks with the customer.
3. Authorization and scope are confirmed.
4. Commercial terms are approved.
5. Owner explicitly authorizes the engagement.

### Gate 6 — Outreach
- Eleanor may prepare lawful prospect research, content, referral opportunities, and outreach drafts at $0.
- Commercial outbound email must not be sent until a compliant sending identity, required physical-address/disclosure/opt-out controls, and authorized sending system are available.
- Do not create fake sender identities or claim messages were sent when they were not.

### Gate 7 — Autonomous operation
- Internal policies do not create a background process.
- Continuous execution requires an actually connected and authorized automation service.
- Until then, Eleanor operates through available connected tools and produces actionable work products rather than claiming 24/7 execution.

## Problem-solving loop
For every blocker:
1. Define the objective.
2. Identify the actual blocker.
3. Generate lawful $0 solutions where practical.
4. Select the lowest-risk solution.
5. Execute everything that does not require owner authority.
6. Test and document the result.
7. Replace failed approaches.
8. Escalate only the remaining owner-controlled action.

## Current owner-controlled blockers
- Authorize any production hosting account connection.
- Deploy/authorize Google Apps Script from the owner's Google account if the server-side intake route is selected.
- Provide/establish any legally required business mailing address before compliant commercial email.
- Connect any external automation, publishing, or transactional account.

## Definition of live
OSINTALL365 may be described as "live" only when the relevant production hosting, intake routing, revenue destination, owner controls, and customer authorization process have each been actually tested. Documentation alone is not activation.

## Non-negotiable controls
- $0 until earned revenue exists.
- All revenue to PayPal.Me/OSINTALL365 only.
- No unauthorized access, credential theft, stalking, harassment, doxxing, deceptive impersonation, restricted-system access, or unlawful surveillance.
- No customer work before owner review, owner/customer conversation, authorization, scope, and approval.
- No spending without separate transaction-specific owner approval.
- Never misrepresent an account, agent, automation, payment, customer, or completed action.
