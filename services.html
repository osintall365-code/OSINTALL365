EXAMPLE FINDINGS

We look for the connections others miss.

Employee exposure: a work badge, uniform, facility photograph, schedule, or security identifier appears on a public social-media page.

Physical exposure: an unsecured or propped door creates an access path toward sensitive areas, equipment, records, or systems.

Digital exposure: public-facing information reveals technology, personnel, vendors, locations, contact details, or other intelligence that increases risk.

AUTHORIZED SCOPE

Professional boundaries matter.

OSINTALL only performs digital diagnostics, testing, collection, and analysis within an authorized scope. We do not provide unauthorized access to accounts, devices, private databases, restricted law-enforcement systems, credentials, or other non-public information. Services are not a substitute for law enforcement, emergency response, legal advice, or a licensed security function where one is required.
