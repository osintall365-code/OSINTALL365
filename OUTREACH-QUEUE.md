# OSINTALL365 Outreach Queue

## Purpose
Prepare qualified business prospects and compliant outreach without spending money or bypassing owner approval.

## Owner control

All outbound commercial outreach must remain within the owner's approved marketing strategy. Agents may research prospects, organize public business information, score relevance, and draft messages. They must not make commitments, negotiate pricing, promise availability, or accept an engagement.

Every prospect that responds with a work request becomes a **NEW → OWNER REVIEW → OWNER CALL REQUIRED** lead.

## Prospect priorities

Focus on organizations that may reasonably benefit from:

- Digital-exposure reviews
- Physical-security observations
- Employee-safety assessments
- Public-source intelligence reviews
- Internal-investigation support where lawful and authorized
- Monitoring and exposure-awareness services

## Qualification fields

- Organization
- Industry
- Public website
- Public business contact channel
- Why OSINTALL365 may be relevant
- Specific publicly observable issue
- Source URL(s)
- Date reviewed
- Outreach status
- Opt-out status
- Owner review status

Do not collect unnecessary personal information.
Do not use restricted databases, stolen data, credentials, or unauthorized access.

## Outreach status

- RESEARCH
- OWNER REVIEW
- APPROVED TO CONTACT
- DRAFT READY
- SENT
- REPLIED
- OWNER CALL REQUIRED
- QUALIFIED
- DECLINED
- OPTED OUT
- CLOSED

## Email compliance gate

Commercial email must comply with applicable law and platform rules. The FTC states that CAN-SPAM applies to commercial email, including business-to-business messages, and requires accurate headers/subject lines, identification of the commercial nature of the message, a valid physical postal address, and a clear opt-out mechanism. Opt-out requests must be honored promptly. See the FTC compliance guide before any campaign is launched.

Do not use deceptive subject lines or sender identities.
Do not send to recipients who have opted out.
Do not conceal the sender.
Do not scrape or harvest addresses through prohibited means.
Do not launch high-volume campaigns from a personal Gmail account merely because the technical sending limit permits it.

## Current sending limitation

No outbound email automation is active yet. The current Gmail connector is unavailable, and the temporary Gmail aliases are routing aliases rather than separate mailboxes. A connected email/CRM provider is required for reliable automated outbound execution.

## Recommended first campaign

Start with a small, highly relevant, owner-reviewed prospect set rather than mass outreach. Each message should identify why the organization was selected, explain the relevant service briefly, provide the OSINTALL365 consultation path, and include an easy way to decline further marketing messages.

## No-spend rule

No purchased lead lists, paid advertising, paid prospecting databases, paid email provider, paid automation, or other outbound expense may be incurred without a separate owner approval for the specific transaction.
