OPERATING WORKFLOWS

Structured intake. Controlled scope. Reviewed delivery.

OSINTALL365 uses a staged workflow so customer requests can be evaluated, authorized, assigned, reviewed, and delivered without exposing unnecessary personal information.

01

Customer Intake

Capture only the information needed to understand the organization, concern, requested service, desired outcome, urgency, and authorization status.

02

Lead Triage

Categorize requests as physical security, employee safety, digital exposure, OSINT, internal investigation, monitoring, or other. Flag requests that require clarification or cannot be accepted.

03

Scope Review

Confirm lawful authority, boundaries, deliverables, limitations, effort, and proposed commercial terms before substantive work begins.

04

Payment Gate

After an engagement is approved, the customer may pay through the official OSINTALL365 PayPal.Me destination. Incoming customer funds may be accepted without separate approval for each receipt.

05

Case Workflow

Approved and paid matters receive a case record, tasks, source trail, findings, quality review, delivery status, and payment status.

06

Report & Delivery

Deliver a structured report containing the executive summary, scope, methodology, findings, risk assessment, source trail, recommendations, and limitations.

07

Close & Follow Up

Record delivery and payment status, close the matter, and identify optional lawful monitoring or follow-up services.

08

Outgoing Funds Gate

No OSINTALL365 agent may independently spend business funds. Every outgoing payment, purchase, subscription, refund, reimbursement, transfer, advertising expense, or other disbursement requires a separate owner decision for that specific transaction.

FINANCIAL CONTROL

Incoming cash may be received. Outgoing cash requires individual approval.

The approved customer payment destination is PayPal.Me/OSINTALL365. Incoming customer payments are part of normal operations. They do not authorize any outgoing expenditure.
