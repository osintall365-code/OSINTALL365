OPERATING WORKFLOWS

Structured intake. Owner-controlled acceptance. Reviewed delivery.

OSINTALL365 uses a staged workflow so every customer request is received, routed, personally reviewed by the owner, discussed with the customer, authorized, assigned, reviewed, and delivered without exposing unnecessary personal information.

AGENT ROUTING

Dedicated operational identities

Agent mailboxes may handle operational processing, but they do not replace the owner. Every work-order request is also routed to the owner for review, and no agent may accept a customer or begin substantive work before the owner personally speaks with the customer and approves the engagement.

01

Work-Order Intake

Capture only the information needed to understand the organization, concern, requested service, desired outcome, urgency, and authorization status. Every work order is simultaneously routed for owner review.

02

Owner Customer Review

The owner personally reviews every prospective customer before the engagement can proceed.

03

Owner Customer Conversation

The owner personally speaks with the customer before any work begins. Agents may prepare a briefing and questions, but may not substitute for this conversation.

04

Agent Triage

Eleanor can categorize the request, identify missing information, flag prohibited or questionable requests, and prepare a concise owner briefing. Triage does not constitute acceptance.

05

Scope Review

Confirm lawful authority, boundaries, deliverables, limitations, effort, and proposed commercial terms after the owner conversation.

06

Owner Approval

The owner explicitly approves or declines the engagement. No customer, agent, or automated workflow can infer approval from silence, payment, or submission of a work order.

07

Payment Pending

After owner approval, the customer receives the approved payment instructions. Payment is never an automatic authorization to expand scope.

08

Case Activation

Only after owner approval and any required payment confirmation does the case become active. Work is limited to the specifically approved scope.

09

Quality Review

Review findings, source trail, scope compliance, limitations, and report quality before delivery.

10

Delivery & Close

Deliver the approved work product, record delivery and payment status, close the matter, and identify any separately approved follow-up work.

11

Outgoing Funds Gate

No OSINTALL365 agent may independently spend business funds. Every outgoing payment, purchase, subscription, refund, reimbursement, transfer, advertising expense, or other disbursement requires a separate owner decision for that specific transaction.

CONTROL BOUNDARIES

Automation supports operations without replacing authorization.

AI assistance can organize, compare, summarize, monitor, and analyze information at scale. It does not independently accept customers, conduct the required owner conversation, approve engagements, expand scope, make material pricing decisions, authorize spending, hire personnel, issue refunds, or perform unauthorized access.
