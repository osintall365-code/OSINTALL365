OPERATING WORKFLOWS

Structured intake. Controlled scope. Reviewed delivery.

OSINTALL365 uses a staged workflow so customer requests can be evaluated, authorized, assigned, reviewed, and delivered without exposing unnecessary personal information.

01

Customer Intake

Capture only the information needed to understand the organization, concern, requested service, desired outcome, urgency, and authorization status.

02

Lead Triage

Categorize requests as physical security, employee safety, digital exposure, OSINT, internal investigation, monitoring, or other. Flag requests that require clarification or cannot be accepted.

03

Scope Review

Confirm lawful authority, boundaries, deliverables, limitations, effort, and proposed commercial terms before substantive work begins.

04

Case Workflow

Approved matters receive a case record, tasks, source trail, findings, quality review, delivery status, and payment status.

05

Report & Delivery

Deliver a structured report containing the executive summary, scope, methodology, findings, risk assessment, source trail, recommendations, and limitations.

06

Close & Follow Up

Record delivery and payment status, close the matter, and identify optional lawful monitoring or follow-up services.

CONTROL GATES

Human approval remains required for consequential decisions.

AI assistance can organize, compare, summarize, monitor, and analyze information at scale. It does not independently approve engagements, expand scope, make material pricing decisions, or perform unauthorized access.
