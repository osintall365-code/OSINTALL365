PRIVACY

Privacy matters.

This initial website is a static information site. Research requests are initiated through the user's email client and sent to osintall365@gmail.com. OSINTALL does not ask customers to submit passwords, payment-card details, or unnecessary sensitive personal information through this site.

Research data

Information supplied for a research request is used to evaluate and perform the requested lawful public-source research, communicate with the customer, and provide the resulting work. Retention, access, and deletion practices may be expanded as the business adds operational systems.

Public sources

OSINTALL research may reference publicly available websites, records, news, social-media content, corporate information, and other open sources. Public availability does not eliminate applicable legal or contractual restrictions, and OSINTALL will not intentionally bypass access controls.

This page is a general business privacy notice, not legal advice. The policy should be reviewed and expanded with counsel before large-scale collection or automated processing of personal information.
