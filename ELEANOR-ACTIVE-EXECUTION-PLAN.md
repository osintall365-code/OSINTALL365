# Eleanor — Active Execution Plan

## Status
**Active internal operating plan.** No spending is authorized by this document.

## Objective
Build OSINTALL365 into a lawful, privacy-conscious, zero-cost-first service business while keeping the owner in control of every customer engagement and every outgoing dollar.

## Workstreams

### 1. Revenue
- Develop tightly scoped, easy-to-understand service offers.
- Prioritize opportunities that can be fulfilled with lawful public/open information and customer-authorized materials.
- Build referral and partnership opportunities that do not require paid advertising.
- Develop recurring monitoring concepts only for owner-approved customers.
- Never claim revenue until an authorized payment system confirms receipt.

### 2. Lead Development
- Maintain the lead queue with organization, public contact route, business need, likely fit, lawful outreach route, and status.
- Prefer high-fit prospects over mass outreach.
- Do not send commercial outreach until the compliance gate and owner-approved sending setup are satisfied.
- Prepare drafts so the owner can review and send when needed.

### 3. Website
- Keep public business information intentionally general.
- Do not publish the owner's personal identifying information.
- Keep owner-review, authorization, privacy, and prohibited-activity language visible.
- Maintain the existing GitHub Pages staging site until a compliant production host is authorized.
- Cloudflare Pages remains the preferred documented zero-cost production migration path, subject to owner account authorization.

### 4. Intake / Email
- Keep `osintall365@gmail.com` as the public business inbox.
- Temporary `+` aliases are routing labels only; do not describe them as separate accounts.
- Keep the Google Apps Script intake bridge prepared, but do not claim it is live until deployed by the owner.
- Every work-order request must reach the owner.

### 5. Content / Social
- Prepare educational, trust-building, and service-explanation content without using personal information.
- No automated publishing is claimed until a compliant publishing account is actually connected.
- Avoid deceptive claims, fabricated customers, fabricated results, or unsupported credentials.

### 6. Customer Operations
The mandatory sequence is:
1. Receive request.
2. Route it to the owner.
3. Owner personally reviews the customer/request.
4. Owner personally speaks with the customer.
5. Confirm authorization, scope, and commercial terms.
6. Owner explicitly approves the engagement.
7. Only then begin substantive work.

### 7. Finance
- Incoming payment destination: PayPal.Me/OSINTALL365.
- Payment does not authorize work.
- No outgoing spending is permitted without a separate, explicit owner approval for the exact transaction.
- Approval format: `May OSINTALL spend $___ to [recipient/vendor] for [specific purpose], [one-time/recurring]?`

## Current Blockers Requiring External Owner Action
1. **Production hosting:** Cloudflare account authorization is required to migrate the site from GitHub Pages.
2. **Live intake bridge:** The owner must deploy/authorize the Google Apps Script from the owner's Google account before it can be treated as active.
3. **Commercial outbound email:** A compliant sending identity/address/opt-out mechanism and owner-approved sending setup are required before commercial outreach is sent.
4. **Social publishing:** A connected publishing account is required before automated posting can be represented as active.
5. **Independent agent mailboxes:** These are not created merely by using Gmail `+` aliases; actual separate accounts would require a supported provider/account setup.
6. **24/7 execution:** A real external automation runner is required. Repository documents alone do not create a background process.

## Non-Negotiable Stop Conditions
Stop and escalate for owner decision if an action would:
- spend or commit money;
- accept a customer;
- begin substantive customer work;
- expand scope;
- materially change pricing/terms;
- hire or contract;
- connect an account with transactional/publishing authority;
- access restricted information without authorization;
- violate law/platform rules;
- expose the owner's personal information;
- make a material legal, financial, security, or reputation-sensitive representation.

## Success Definition
OSINTALL365 is considered operationally successful only when the system can reliably receive leads, route every lead to the owner, preserve the owner approval gates, convert approved engagements into lawful work, receive authorized customer payments, and improve its workflows without bypassing controls.
