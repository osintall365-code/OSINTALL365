# OSINTALL365 Outbound Compliance Gate

## Purpose
Prevent the revenue operation from creating legal, privacy, spam, or platform risk while still allowing the business to prepare high-quality outreach.

## Current status
**COMMERCIAL OUTBOUND EMAIL: BLOCKED** until the required sending infrastructure and compliance information are available.

## Required before activation

1. Accurate sender identity and reply address.
2. Valid physical postal address that can lawfully be included in commercial messages.
3. Clear and conspicuous opt-out mechanism.
4. Working suppression process so opted-out recipients are not re-mailed.
5. Authorized sending system/account.
6. Owner-approved outreach policy and target criteria.
7. No deceptive subject lines, sender information, or claims.
8. Prospect data limited to information reasonably needed for the lawful business purpose.

## What agents may do now

- Research organizations using public sources.
- Identify business-relevant security/OSINT problems.
- Prepare a concise prospect brief.
- Draft a proposed message for owner review.
- Record why the prospect is relevant.
- Flag uncertainty, compliance concerns, or missing information.
- Build an owner-review queue.

## What agents may not do now

- Send commercial email.
- Scrape or acquire restricted/private data.
- Circumvent platform controls.
- Pretend to be the owner or another person.
- Promise services, prices, timelines, or outcomes.
- Add prospects to an automated campaign without the required compliance controls.

## Owner review record

Each outbound opportunity should contain:

- Organization
- Public business website/source
- Business-relevant reason for contact
- Proposed service category
- Evidence supporting relevance
- Proposed contact method
- Compliance concerns
- Owner decision: CONTACT / DO NOT CONTACT / NEED MORE RESEARCH
- Owner notes

## Stop rule

If any required compliance element is missing, the workflow stops at preparation and escalates to the owner. The agent must find a lawful no-cost alternative rather than bypassing the requirement.
